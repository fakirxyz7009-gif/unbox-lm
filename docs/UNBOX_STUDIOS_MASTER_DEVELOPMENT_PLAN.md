### Latest acceptance update — 2026-11-12 durable active-audio take journal

- Armed audio recording now creates a separate IndexedDB `recordingJournal` before the AudioWorklet starts. Worklet blocks are copied on the main thread and committed in bounded batches; the journal records the confirmed record edge, original input-latency compensation, and the last durable frame. Project saves clear a completed journal only when the saved JSON contains every placed audio asset, so an older queued save cannot erase a newer recovery copy.
- `npm run test:browser-recording-journal` passed in Chrome: four known stereo blocks (16,384 frames) were recovered into a new audio track, sample order and channels were checked, the project was saved and reopened, and interrupted/completed journals were cleared. The exact-frame recorder probe also passed with the journal observer receiving all three matching blocks.
- Scope remains intentionally honest: a crash can lose the newest uncommitted batch; active MIDI-only takes, master-output capture and zero-loss crash recovery are not claimed. Audio journals from another project are not silently inserted into the open song, and a journal without a confirmed record edge is refused.

### Latest audio-origin and model-separation hardening — 2026-11-11

- Every project-audio asset now gets an explicit bounded origin record. Local files and SF2 zones become `user-file` with `unknown` rights rather than silently appearing owned; live captures are private `user-recording` assets; freezes, renders, note captures, edits and source-separated stems retain a derivative/app-render record. Verified recorded stem-library assets retain their declared source, licence and credit. The record survives IndexedDB and `.unbox` archive paths, but it is not independent licence verification or authorization to redistribute source audio.
- The Fast Local heuristic splitter remains the default. Spleeter/Demucs is a trained but non-generative local source-separation option: it is never auto-selected merely because it is installed, requires a per-session acknowledgement in the UI, and the client plus loopback route refuse it without consent. It can produce artifacts/bleed and never turns its output into a verified human recording or licensed replacement sound. This strengthens the user's no-AI-audio preference without falsely calling a source-derived transform audio generation.

## Completion rules

A phase closes only when its promised behavior has a concrete implementation, focused automated coverage where practical, and evidence from the real execution path. A simulated controller or offline native fixture cannot stand in for physical hardware or live DAW hosting. Record the machine, device, browser/audio backend, exact procedure, result and known limits for manual acceptance runs.

Keep sound provenance accurate: Gemini may assist with symbolic rack plans, parameter values or text, while the Web Audio graph produces sound. Algorithmic synthesis is not AI-generated audio; a recorded sample is not proof of a known performer. Keep proxy plugin DSP clearly labelled. Use only recordings that are owned or redistributable under their license and required attribution.
